From a CAFANS listing to a SuperBuy order

  1. Open a CAFANS product page, select Buy Link and choose SuperBuy if it is available. If the agent link fails, copy the original seller URL and check whether SuperBuy can open it.
  2. Compare the seller’s measurements with an item you already own. Save the selected color, size and quantity before placing the order.
  3. When warehouse QC photos are available, compare the delivered item with your saved variant. Confirm your shipping quote using the destination and the packed parcel details.

SuperBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a SuperBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping shortlist that groups product links and reference prices so you can compare items before opening their seller listings through SuperBuy. This independent guide uses CAFANS listings.

What should I check in warehouse QC photos?

Match the photos to your selected variant and quantity. Look for the expected color, size label and visible condition. If fit is uncertain, check whether you can request a measurement photo before dispatch.

Why can my shipping quote change?

A route may calculate its charge from actual weight or volumetric weight. Packing changes can alter the parcel dimensions, so use the packed weight and dimensions when comparing final quotes.

Do the catalog prices include delivery?

The listed amounts are item-only estimates. Confirm the current price for your selected variant and add delivery, service fees and any applicable import charges separately.
